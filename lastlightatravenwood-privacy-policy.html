Last Light at Ravenwood - Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Last Light at Ravenwood by Tristonia (“we”, “our”, or “us”) handles your information when you use our mobile game.

Last Updated: 6th of November 2026

Last Light at Ravenwood has no account, no server of its own and no internet access. The names you type in and everything that happens in a game stay on your phone and are forgotten when the game ends.

1. About Last Light at Ravenwood

Last Light at Ravenwood is a party game for 4 to 12 people who share one phone. Players are secretly dealt roles, take turns picking and voting in private, and try to work out who the culprits are. It is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal data. There is no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code in the game that sends information anywhere. The game does not ask for the internet permission, so it cannot send data off your phone.

3. Player Names and Game Data

You type in a name for each player at the start of a game. These names, the roles dealt, the picks and the votes are held in the phone’s memory only while the game is running. They are not saved to storage, not backed up and not shared. Closing the game clears them.

4. Permissions

  • Vibration: a short buzz when you press a button. The game asks for nothing else, and it does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or location.

While a game is in progress the game keeps the screen awake so the phone does not lock as it is passed around the table.

5. Data Sharing

We don’t share or sell data, because we don’t collect any.

6. Children’s Privacy

The game is rated for a general audience and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, the changes will be posted on this page with a revised “Last Updated” date.
