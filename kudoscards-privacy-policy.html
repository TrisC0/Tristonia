Kudos Cards - Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Kudos Cards by Tristonia (“we”, “our”, or “us”) handles your information when you use our mobile application.

Last Updated: 11th of October 2026

Kudos Cards has no account, no server and no internet access. Everything your team puts into it stays on the device it was entered on. We never see any of it.

1. About Kudos Cards

Kudos Cards is a task board for a team. Finished tasks earn points, points earn Bronze, Silver and Gold cards, and cards unlock perks that the team’s admin sets. It is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal data. There is no sign-in, no analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising in the app. The app does not ask for permission to use the internet, so it cannot send anything anywhere.

3. What the App Stores on Your Device

To work, the app keeps the following on the device:

  • The team name, and the names and roles of the people added to the team
  • Tasks, their notes, comments and history
  • Points, kudos messages, cards earned and perks redeemed
  • Settings such as the clean-up schedule
  • A hashed form of each person’s PIN (the PIN itself is not stored)

This data is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so it does not leave the device that way either. Done tasks are archived or deleted automatically after the period the team’s admin chooses. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, removes everything.

4. Permissions

  • Vibrate: for small haptic touches, such as when points are awarded.

The app also reads the phone’s motion sensor to tilt the shine on the cards. The readings are used on screen at that moment and are not stored.

5. Data Sharing

We don’t share or sell data, because we don’t collect any.

6. Children’s Privacy

Kudos Cards is made for teams at work and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

A later version may add an optional team sync so several phones can share one board. If it does, this page will be updated before that version is released, with a revised “Last Updated” date.
