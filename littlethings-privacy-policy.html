The Little Things - Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how The Little Things by Tristonia (“we”, “our”, or “us”) handles your information when you use our mobile application.

Last Updated: 11th of October 2026

The Little Things has no account, no server and no internet access. Everything you put into it stays on your phone. We don’t see your moments, your notes or your photos.

1. About The Little Things

The Little Things gives you one small thing to notice each day, through sight, touch, smell, sound or taste, and a journal to keep the moments you like. It is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal data. The app does not have permission to use the internet, so it cannot send anything to us or to anyone else. There is no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising.

3. Photos

When you add a photo to a moment, you either choose one with Android’s photo picker or take one with your phone’s camera app. The app only sees the photo you pick or take, and keeps a copy of it in its own private storage on your phone. It does not ask for permission to read your photo library or use the camera directly. Deleting a moment deletes its copy of the photo.

4. Data Storage

Your moments, notes, photos and settings are stored only on your device. The app is excluded from Android’s cloud backup, so none of it goes to your Google account. If you move to a new phone using a cable or a direct phone-to-phone transfer, Android may bring it across. Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on the device.

5. Permissions

  • Notifications: for the gentle nudges, if you turn them on. Optional.
  • Vibrate: for a small haptic touch when the timer ends.
  • Run at startup, keep awake, foreground service and network state: added by Android’s WorkManager library so that scheduled nudges still arrive after your phone restarts. The app has no internet permission, so network state is only read by that library and nothing is sent.

6. Data Sharing

We don’t share or sell data, because we don’t collect any and the app has no way to send it.

7. Children’s Privacy

The Little Things is not designed for children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, the changes will be posted on this page with a revised “Last Updated” date.
