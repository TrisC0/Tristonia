About

One person, building apps.

I'm Tris. I've worked in technology for over 24 years, and Tristonia is the studio where I turn my ideas into Android apps.

Who I am

Welcome to Tristonia.

I like solving problems, and I like automating the boring bits even more. Most of these apps started that way. Please have a look around, and I hope you find one that's useful to you.

How I work

It starts with an idea.

Most of my apps start with something small that bugs me, or an idea I keep coming back to. I build it, use it on my own phone until it feels right, and then put it on Google Play.

I try to keep each app to the one thing it's for, so it does what it says without wasting your time.

What I care about

Four things I try
to get right every time.

01
Creativity
I don't want to make another app that looks like all the others. If there's a more fun way to do something, that's the one I go for.
→
02
Simplicity
You shouldn't need a tutorial. If you have to stop and think about how to use it, then I haven't got it right yet.
→
03
Privacy
Your data is yours. Wherever I can, everything stays on your phone, and most of the apps don't need an account at all.
→
04
Quality
I would rather spend an extra week on something than put it out before it feels finished.
