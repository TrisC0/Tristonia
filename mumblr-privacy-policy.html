Mumblr - Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Mumblr by Tristonia (“we”, “our”, or “us”) handles your information when you use our mobile application.

Last Updated: 6th of November 2026

Mumblr has no internet access, no account and no server of its own. Your recordings and their text stay on your phone. We never see them.

1. About Mumblr

Mumblr records your voice, or takes an audio file you give it, and turns the speech into text on your phone. It is free to use, with an optional one-time purchase called Mumblr Pro. There are no ads.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal data. There is no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising in the app. The app does not have the Android permission to use the internet, so it cannot send your audio or text anywhere.

3. The Microphone

The microphone is used only while you are recording, after you press Record. A notification is shown for as long as a recording is running. The audio is saved on your device. It is not sent anywhere.

4. Audio You Import

When you share an audio file to Mumblr, or pick one with the Import button, Mumblr reads that one file, makes a copy in its own private storage and transcribes the copy. It does not read any other files.

5. Transcription

Speech is turned into text by a speech model that runs on your device. The model is delivered to your phone by Google Play after you install the app, so Mumblr itself never needs to connect to the internet.

6. Data Storage

Your recordings, transcripts and settings are stored in the app’s private storage on your device. Android may include app data in your device backup to your own Google account if you have backup turned on. Deleting a recording in Mumblr removes its audio and text from the device. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored.

When you use Share or Export, Mumblr hands the text to the app you choose. This only happens when you ask for it, and where the text goes from there is up to you.

7. Mumblr Pro and Google Play

Mumblr Pro is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment and tells the app whether you own Pro. We receive no payment details, name or email address. Google’s own privacy policy applies to the purchase: policies.google.com/privacy

8. Permissions

  • Microphone: to record your voice, as described above.
  • Notifications: to show that a recording or a transcription is running.
  • Foreground service: so a recording or transcription keeps running when you switch to another app.

9. Data Sharing

We don’t share or sell data, because we don’t collect any.

10. Children’s Privacy

Mumblr is not designed for children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

11. Open Source and Models

Mumblr uses open-source speech software and models. Their licences are listed in the app under Settings, Open source licences.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, the changes will be posted on this page with a revised “Last Updated” date.
