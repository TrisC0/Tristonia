Apps with character.

Thirteen apps so far. Each one started as something I couldn't stop thinking about, and I kept going until I would happily use it on my own phone.

Nine Pocket
Android · Magic: The Gathering Binders
01 / 13

"Every card in a sleeved pocket, in binders you carry on your phone."

Nine Pocket keeps your Magic: The Gathering collection in leather binders on your phone, nine cards to a page. Scan a card and it reads the set code and collector number in the corner, so it finds the exact printing you own, not just the name. Swipe to turn the pages, see what every card, page and binder is worth, and build your Commander or Standard decks next to your binders, with the cards you already own and the binder they're in.

Inside the build

Scans the set code and collector number to find the exact printing, with search by name as a fallback

Nine-pocket pages you swipe through, with drag and drop between pockets, pages and binders

Daily prices from Scryfall in pounds, euros or dollars, per card, page, binder and collection

Decks for Commander, Standard, Modern, Pioneer or casual, with have and need counts across your binders

Binder covers in eleven colours and four textures, with an emblem or your own photo

Completely free. No ads, no purchases, no account, and your collection stays on your phone

The Little Things
Android · Mindfulness
02 / 13

"Meditation for people who can't sit still."

Each day The Little Things gives you one small thing to go and notice: the smell of the street you're on, the warmth of a mug in your hands, the sound furthest away from you. No need to close your eyes or find twenty quiet minutes. When you notice something you like, keep it with a few words or a photo, and your moments gather in a little journal you can look back through.

Inside the build

A new prompt every day, moving through sight, touch, smell, sound and taste

150 prompts for home, work, outdoors and anywhere in between

A little journal of the moments you keep, with words, photos or both

A one, three or five minute timer with a soft chime at the end

Up to three gentle nudges a day, and they stay quiet on Do Not Disturb

No streaks, no scores, no account, and no internet access at all

Get it on Google Play Privacy Policy
Intelligent Receipt Scanner
Android · Receipts & Spending
03 / 13

"Tells you before you lose the chance to take something back."

Intelligent Receipt Scanner keeps every receipt you care about on your phone. Point the camera at one and it finds the edges, straightens the page and reads the shop, the date, the total, the VAT and the items for you. Set a return window or a warranty in one tap and it reminds you before it runs out, and your spending sorts itself into categories with monthly totals and a budget.

Inside the build

Scans the receipt, straightens it and reads the shop, date, total, VAT and items

Return windows and warranties set in one tap, with reminders before they run out

Spending sorted into categories, with monthly totals, a six-month chart and a budget

Every receipt in one spreadsheet you can filter, total and export to CSV

Built for UK receipts with pounds, day-first dates and VAT, and works with other currencies

Read on your phone, not in the cloud. No account and no internet permission

Get it on Google Play Privacy Policy
Cartridge
Android · Game Collection
04 / 13

"Your game shelf, lit up like a shop."

Cartridge turns the games on your shelves into a late-night game shop on your phone. Scan the barcode on the back of a case and it finds the game, its platform and its box art, then stands it under that platform's neon sign with a spine that looks just like the case. No barcode? Take a photo of the box or cartridge and it reads the title for you. Loose cartridges stand face out. Tap a game to track whether you've beaten it, what condition it's in and what you paid.

Inside the build

Barcode scanning on the phone, plus a photo reader for boxes and cartridges with no barcode

Over 60 platforms, from the ZX Spectrum, Amstrad and Atari 2600 to PS5 and Switch 2

Spines drawn in code with the proper platform banner and true-to-life case sizes

Loose cartridges drawn as the cart itself, with the box art or your own photo on the label

Play status, condition, price paid and a wishlist, with a spreadsheet export

Completely free. No ads, no purchases, no account, and your collection stays on your phone

Get it on Google Play Privacy Policy

Cubby

Bronze, Silver and Gold cards drawn in code, with a metal shine that follows the tilt of your phone

Perks set by the team's admin, redeemed with a code and handed over in person

Share a new card or a win to any app on your phone

Epafi
Android · Keep in Touch
08 / 13

"A kind little nudge to keep in touch."

Epafi (επαφή, Greek for contact, or touch) is for the people you'd hate to drift from. Put them in circles like family or old friends, each with its own rhythm, and Epafi keeps a gentle eye on how long it's been. Once a day at most, it tells you who might like a hello, and saying hello is one tap away.

Inside the build

Circles with their own rhythm, and a ring on each person that fills as their time comes round

One-tap call, text, WhatsApp, Signal or Telegram, and it asks if you caught up when you come back

A daily nudge with "Caught up" and "Later this week" right on the notification

Birthdays from your contacts, reminded three days before and on the day

A home screen widget and a backup file you can lock with a passphrase

Completely free. No ads, no account, and no internet access, so your team's details stay on your phone

Play Games achievements and leaderboards to chase
