Grovestead - Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Grovestead by Tristonia LTD (“we”, “our”, or “us”) handles your information when you use our mobile application (package com.tristonia.grovestead).

Effective date: 26th of October 2026

Grovestead has no account, no sign-in and no chat. Your garden, stars and settings stay on your phone. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect some device information to show them.

1. About Grovestead

Grovestead is a free Queens-style brain puzzle. Each puzzle you finish helps grow a woodland homestead. There is no account, no sign-in, no chat and no content shared between players.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal data ourselves. We have no server for the game and we never see your progress. The only third parties involved are Google AdMob, which shows the ads, and Google Play, which handles the optional purchase. Both are described below.

3. Game Progress and Storage

Your garden, stars and settings are stored only on your phone. If you have Android backup switched on, Android may include them in its backup to your own Google account, so they can be restored on a new phone. Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on the device.

4. Ads

The free version shows a full-screen ad between puzzles, roughly one every five finished puzzles. Ads are served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect and use your device’s advertising ID, your IP address, information about your device and the app, and your approximate location. Google explains how it uses this information here: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

If you are in the UK, the EU or the EEA, the app first shows Google’s consent form (the User Messaging Platform) and only asks for ads once the consent rules allow it.

You are in control of this:

  • Change your consent choices: you can review or change them from the app at any time.
  • Reset your advertising ID: in Android settings, under Privacy, then Ads, you can reset or delete your advertising ID.
  • Remove ads: the one-time “Remove ads” upgrade turns the ads off for good.

5. Purchases

Grovestead offers one optional in-app purchase, “Remove ads”, which is a one-time payment made through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment, and we never see your card or payment details.

6. Sharing Your Garden

The share button saves a picture of your garden and opens the Android share sheet, so you choose where it goes. The app itself does not upload the picture anywhere.

7. Permissions

  • Internet access: to load and show ads.
  • Network state: so the ads can check whether you are online.
  • Google Play billing: for the optional “Remove ads” purchase.

Grovestead does not ask for your camera, microphone, contacts or precise location.

8. Data Sharing

We don’t sell or share any data, because we don’t collect any. The information AdMob uses to show ads goes to Google under Google’s own policy, linked above.

9. Children’s Privacy

Grovestead is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, the changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
