Nine Pocket - Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Nine Pocket by Tristonia (“we”, “our”, or “us”) handles your information when you use our mobile application.

Last Updated: 12th of October 2026

Nine Pocket has no account and no server of its own. Your collection lives on your device. We never see your cards, your binders or what they are worth.

1. About Nine Pocket

Nine Pocket lets you scan a trading card with your camera, or search for it by name, and keep it in virtual nine-pocket binders on your phone, with card prices. It is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal data. There is no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising in the app.

3. The Camera

The camera is used while the scanner is open, to read the name, set code and collector number printed on a card. The camera image is processed on your device by Google’s ML Kit text recognition and is not saved or sent anywhere.

4. Looking Up Cards and Prices

When you scan or search for a card, Nine Pocket sends the text it read or the text you typed (a card name, or a set code and collector number) to Scryfall to find the card, its image and its price. Nine Pocket also asks Scryfall for updated prices for the cards in your collection, at most once a day or when you tap “Refresh now”. Those requests contain only Scryfall’s card identifiers, not your binders, quantities or anything about you. Scryfall’s privacy policy: scryfall.com/docs/privacy

Card images are downloaded from Scryfall. Like any web request, these carry your IP address. No account, name or other details from the app are included.

5. Google ML Kit

The card reader is Google’s ML Kit, which runs on your device. ML Kit may send Google basic diagnostic information so Google can keep it working well: device model and Android version, the app’s package name and version, a per-installation identifier that is not meant to identify you, and performance and error figures. It does not send camera images. Google describes this here: developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure

6. Data Storage

Your binders, cards, conditions, quantities, prices and settings are stored on your device. If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include them in your device backup to your own Google account, so your collection comes back on a new phone. Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on the device.

If you use CSV export, Nine Pocket creates a file of your collection in the place you choose. Where it goes from there is up to you. CSV import reads only the file you pick.

7. Permissions

  • Camera: to read cards, as described above.
  • Internet: to look up cards, prices and card images.
  • Vibrate: for small haptic touches, such as page turns. You can turn these off in Settings.

8. Data Sharing

We don’t share or sell data, because we don’t collect any. The only things that leave your device are the card and price lookups and ML Kit diagnostics described above.

9. Children’s Privacy

Nine Pocket does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.
